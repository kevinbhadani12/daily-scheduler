Every trading day after the Indian market closes, prepare a short stock market update for a complete beginner.

Cover:
1. How the market did today: Nifty 50 and Sensex closing level, change in points and percent. Explain in one line what an up or down day of this size means.
2. Biggest movers: 3–5 large Indian companies whose shares rose or fell the most, and the real reason why (results, news, policy). If the reason is unclear, say so.
3. What moved the market: the 2–3 main causes today (for example global markets, oil prices, the rupee, RBI or government decisions, foreign investor buying or selling). Explain each cause and how it connects to share prices.
4. Global snapshot: US markets (S&P 500, Nasdaq) from their last session, and any major Asian or European move, in a small table.
5. One concept explained: pick one idea from today's news (for example P/E ratio, FII, market cap, dividend, IPO) and explain it with a simple rupee example.
6. What to watch tomorrow: scheduled events such as company results, economic data releases, or policy meetings.

Rules:
- Use simple, short sentences. Expand every abbreviation the first time.
- Use rupee examples, such as "if you had invested ₹10,000…".
- Only report real numbers from reliable sources (NSE, BSE, Reuters, Economic Times, Moneycontrol, Business Standard, Mint). Link each source.
- This is education, not investment advice. Never tell the reader to buy or sell anything.
- If the market was closed today (holiday), say so briefly and give only the global snapshot and one concept.
- Aim for a 4–5 minute read.
